Labs

Live classroom tools: students enter measurements on their phones and the class watches the results build up on the room's screen. Each lab has a student form and an instructor dashboard.

BIO 151 · Live Spectrophotometry

Chlorophyll absorption spectrum — absorbance versus wavelength, one line per lab group.

Fall 2026: this lab is finished. Data entry is closed. Groups can still open the student form to see their saved readings, and the dashboard still shows the class graph. The lab opens again next semester.

Student form

Join your group, enter absorbance readings, come back and edit them. This is the QR-code link in the lab packet.

Classroom dashboard

Live graph, group completion status, data table, and instructor tools (names, CSV export, wavelengths).

BIO 151 · Mitosis

Count cells in each stage of the cell cycle in two regions, 25 cells per region. The class sees the totals and the mitotic index; only the instructor sees names.

Student form

Enter your name and your counts for each region, and save. You can come back and edit on the same device.

Classroom dashboard

Class totals by stage and the mitotic index in each region. No student names.

Instructor

Names, counts, completion and mitotic indices; corrections, access recovery and the named CSV. Needs the instructor key.
