# biologyatscc.com is kept out of search engines for now (Justin Howard's call, 2026-11-16).
# It may go public one day: see "Search engines" in README.md for how to switch it back.
#
# Every HTML page carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">. The pages stay
# crawlable on purpose: a search engine has to read a page to see that tag, and a page
# blocked here that is already in its index would stay listed. Files that can't carry the
# tag (PDFs and pictures) are blocked here instead.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /*.pdf$
Disallow: /*.jpg$
Disallow: /*.jpeg$
Disallow: /*.png$
Disallow: /*.webp$
