How links are checked

Every link is checked by an automated script that identifies itself as this site's link checker. When a site refuses the automated check or cannot be reached, the card is marked ! Needs review and the link is left unchanged for an administrator to confirm in a browser; when a link redirects, the card shows the new destination. Links are never silently replaced.
