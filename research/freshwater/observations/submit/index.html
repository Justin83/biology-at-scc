Submit a Field Observation

Record a site visit from your phone. Attach your photos and the form reads the time and location saved in them. A faculty reviewer checks every entry before anything appears on this website.

This form needs JavaScript

Turn on JavaScript in your browser to use the form. Nothing is sent without it.

Safety first

Check current Kentucky harmful algal bloom advisories (opens in a new tab) before you go. Do not touch scums or mats with bare hands, and never enter water alone. Photograph from the bank.

1 Photos

Up to 6 photos: looking upstream, looking downstream, the stream bed, and close-ups of anything green, blue-green or slimy. No people's faces.

    How the form reads your photos

    Phone cameras save the date, time and often the GPS position inside each photo. The form reads those on your phone and fills in the time and location below — you can change them. It then shrinks each photo (to at most 1600 pixels) and makes a fresh copy without the hidden data, so your phone model and exact position are never published inside the image. The time, position and camera model are kept as separate fields that only the reviewer sees until the entry is approved.

    Many phones remove the location when a photo is shared with a website. If no location is found, use your current location (if you are at the site) or tap the map.
