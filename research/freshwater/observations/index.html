Field Observations

Review and publication

  1. Draft. Students enter their record after the visit.
  2. Submitted. The record goes to a faculty reviewer, who checks units, methods, calibration, photographs and identifications.
  3. Changes requested or approved.
  4. Published. Only approved records appear here and on the map. Records are archived, never deleted, if they are later corrected.

Until the authenticated research workspace is built (Phase 4 of the roadmap), records are kept by faculty outside this public website. Unpublished records and student information are never stored in the site's public code repository.
