Submitting from the field

Students send observations from a phone with the observation form: take or attach up to six photos, and the form reads the date, time and GPS position saved in them (many phones remove the position when sharing with a website, so the form can also use the phone's current location or a point tapped on the map). Then add the site, conditions, any measurements and who was there, and send the entry with the class code.

  • Photos are shrunk and re-saved on the phone without their hidden data (camera model, exact position) before they are sent.
  • Nothing is public until a faculty reviewer approves it. Reviewers remove any photo that shows a recognizable person, license plate or home.
  • Names appear only for people who chose to show theirs; otherwise the credit is "SCC Biology students".

Review and publication

  1. Draft. Students fill in the form; it is saved on their own phone until they send it.
  2. Waiting for review. The entry goes to a private database that this public website cannot read. A faculty reviewer checks units, methods, calibration, photographs and identifications.
  3. Changes requested, approved or rejected.
  4. Published. Only approved entries appear here and on the map. Entries are archived rather than deleted if they are later corrected.

Unpublished entries, review notes, and the names of students who did not choose to show them are never public and are never stored in the site's public code repository. Online submission is switched on by faculty; until then the form runs in practice mode and sends nothing. Faculty use the review page (instructor key required).
