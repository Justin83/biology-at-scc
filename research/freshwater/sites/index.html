Proposing a new site

A new site needs: a name and location from an authoritative source (for example, the USGS National Hydrography Dataset), landowner or agency permission, a documented safe access route, a reason it helps answer a research question, and faculty approval. The research questions suggest the kinds of sites that would be most useful — especially an open agricultural pond to contrast with shaded Allen Branch.
