STEAM Day 2027: Volunteer with the Math & Science Club

Friday, March 5, 2027 · 8:00 AM to about 2:00 PM · North Campus

Sign up to work this event

MSSE scholars: working STEAM Day counts as one of the 4 activities you need each semester.

STEAM Day brings eighth graders from area schools to SCC for hands-on activities in science, technology, engineering, arts, and math. Students explore new ideas, meet educators and professionals, and see where their interests might take them.

Last year, 81 students from 10 regional schools came to campus, and SCC students helped lead activities. See photos and highlights from the 2026 event (SCC news, opens in a new tab).

Why volunteer?

You’ll help younger students feel welcome and get a closer look at what happens at SCC. You’ll also practice communicating, working with others, and sharing your interests. Those skills matter in many fields.

Did you know? The AAMC premed competencies (opens in a new tab) includes service orientation among the competencies for future medical students. Engineering programs accredited by ABET engineering criteria (opens in a new tab) emphasize teamwork, communication, and considering how engineering affects people and society. STEAM Day gives you a chance to practice those skills, whether you’re interested in health care, engineering, education, or another path.
